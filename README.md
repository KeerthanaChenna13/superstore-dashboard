# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive business dashboard created in
**Microsoft Power BI** using the Kaggle Superstore Sales dataset. The
dashboard summarises sales and profitability and helps explore
performance across customer segments, product categories, and regions.

## Objectives

-   Monitor overall sales and profit.
-   Understand sales contribution by customer segment.
-   Compare sales performance across product categories.
-   Examine profit contribution by product subcategory.
-   Compare regional sales quantity distribution.
-   Present business information in a clear visual format to support
    decision-making.

## Tools and Technologies

-   **Microsoft Power BI** --- dashboard creation and data
    visualisation.
-   **Kaggle Superstore Sales dataset** --- source data.
-   **Canva** --- presentation slides.

## Dashboard Contents

The dashboard includes: - KPI cards for Total Sales (**2.33M**) and
Total Profit (**292.30K**). - Total discount by customer segment. -
Profit contribution by product subcategory. - Country and category
performance table. - Sales contribution by customer segment. - Sales
performance by product category. - Regional sales quantity distribution.

## Key Insights

-   Total sales are approximately **2.33M** and total profit is
    approximately **292.30K**.
-   The **Consumer** segment contributes the largest share of sales
    (**50.32%**).
-   **Technology** has the highest category sales (approximately
    **0.8M**).
-   The **West** region contributes the largest share of sales quantity
    (**32.25%**).

These figures are transcribed from the dashboard used for the
presentation. Check them against the final `.pbix` report before
submission if you have made later changes.
